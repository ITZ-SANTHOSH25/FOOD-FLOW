The Platform

One continuous thread from enquiry to audit.

Most food businesses run on disconnected registers, WhatsApp approvals and month-end guesswork. FOODFLOW replaces that with a single operational spine — every hand-off recorded, every number traceable.

01 — Capture

Order Intake

Salesmen raise orders from the field with live pricing, stock availability and customer credit status — no callbacks, no re-keying.

02 — Approve

Review & Confirmation

Admin validates quantities, dispatch windows and payment terms. Approved orders flow straight to the production planner.

03 — Plan

Production Scheduling

Batches are allocated across seven lines with shift, supervisor and planned yield — respecting shelf life and raw material cover.

04 — Produce

Batch & Variance

Actual output, wastage and yield are logged against plan. Any variance beyond tolerance raises an alert the same shift.

05 — Dispatch

Packing & Invoicing

Packed consignments generate GST invoices automatically. Receivables age in real time, by customer and by salesman.

06 — Assure

Audit & Reconciliation

Pre-production, in-process, dispatch, inventory and financial audits close the loop with scored findings and corrective actions.

Why FoodFlow

Enterprise rigour, without the cold.

We built FOODFLOW for a family-run manufacturer in Tiruchirappalli that had outgrown its registers. Every screen is designed around a real hand-off — and every number can be traced back to the person who entered it.

i.

Traceable by design

Order → batch → invoice → audit. Every record keeps its lineage, so a recall or a query takes minutes, not days.

ii.

Variance before it becomes loss

Yield, wastage and stock cover are compared to plan continuously, with alerts routed to the person who can act.

iii.

Made for Indian food operations

GST invoicing, credit terms, shelf-life-sensitive batches, regional distribution and multi-unit warehouses.

iv.

Calm, legible interfaces

Serif headings, warm paper tones and generous spacing — a workspace people are happy to open at 7 a.m.
